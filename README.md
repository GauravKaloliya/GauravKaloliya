## ✦ &nbsp;About

> I’m Gaurav Kaloliya — a Computer Science & Engineering (AI & ML) student at Adani University, obsessed with building intelligent systems that don’t just work, but actually matter.

What started as curiosity about how machines think has evolved into a full-blown mission: turning bold ideas into real, scalable solutions. I’ve built full-stack applications, trained machine learning models that solve practical problems, developed IoT systems, and shipped mobile apps — all while obsessively refining every detail from backend logic to pixel-perfect user experiences. Whether I’m optimizing neural networks for efficiency or designing seamless digital interactions, I thrive on the entire journey from 0 to 1.

I’m especially drawn to AI that creates meaningful impact — be it in healthcare, sustainability, or education. I believe technology should feel almost magical to the end user while being rock-solid behind the scenes.

Beyond technical skills, I bring sharp problem-solving, systems thinking, and a deep sense of empathy. I’ve learned that the best products aren’t just built with code — they’re built with clarity, collaboration, and genuine care for the people who’ll use them.

When I’m not coding or reading research papers, you’ll find me on the football field chasing the ball or lost in pop music playlists. Both keep my mind sharp, my energy high, and my creativity flowing.

### 🧠 &nbsp;C.O.G.N.I.T
**Cognitive Network for Image & Text Modeling**

A **leakage-safe multimodal deep learning framework** predicting cognitive state from **image, text, and behavioral telemetry** (~400 participants).

OpenCLIP and Sentence-Transformer embeddings are fused with **keystroke-dynamics** signals in a **late-fusion neural architecture**, modeling Cognitive Load, Writing Quality, Image–Text Alignment, and Cognitive Strain.

**📈 Up to 0.9787 R² on unseen test data**
